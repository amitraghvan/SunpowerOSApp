# SolarOS Composite Indian GST Taxation Engine

## 1. Statutory Works Contract Principle

In India, rooftop solar EPC contracts are treated as a Composite Supply consisting of goods (modules, inverters, structures) and services (erection, civil work, electrical installation, net-metering commissioning).

Under GST Notification No. 24/2018-Central Tax (Rate) and subsequent circulars:
- **Goods Portion:** 70% of total contract value, taxable at **12% GST** (6% CGST + 6% SGST).
- **Services Portion:** 30% of total contract value, taxable at **18% GST** (9% CGST + 9% SGST).

## 2. Mathematical Effective Rate

$$\text{Effective GST Rate} = (0.70 \times 12\%) + (0.30 \times 18\%) = 8.4\% + 5.4\% = \mathbf{13.80\%}$$

### Example Calculation for ₹2,00,000 Contract:
- **Base EPC Value:** ₹2,00,000
- **Goods Component (70%):** ₹1,40,000 @ 12% = ₹16,800
- **Services Component (30%):** ₹60,000 @ 18% = ₹10,800
- **Total Composite Tax:** ₹27,600
- **Gross Invoice Amount:** ₹2,27,600
