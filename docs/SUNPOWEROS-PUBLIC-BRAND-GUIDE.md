# SunPowerOS Public Brand & Visual Identity Guide

## 1. Product Identity

- **Product Name**: SUNPOWEROS
- **Company**: SUNPOWER SOLUTIONS
- **Brand Descriptor**: Solar EPC Operations Platform
- **Primary Tagline**: **One platform. Every solar operation.**
- **Secondary Tagline**: **Power your solar business. Not your paperwork.**
- **Marketing Positioning Statement**:
  > "SunPowerOS connects your solar business from the first customer enquiry to engineering, commercial operations and project execution."

---

## 2. Logo Design Concept & Artwork

The SunPowerOS brand identity centers around an original geometric solar emblem:
- **Solar Core**: Solid brand plum (`#714B67`) center representing stability, enterprise management, and operational control.
- **Outer Orbit Ring**: Subtle dashed ring (`#E4E5E9`) representing connected business workflows.
- **Cardinal Solar Rays**:
  - North: Coral energy stroke (`#FF747C`)
  - South: Teal efficiency stroke (`#06C6AE`)
  - East: Amber radiance stroke (`#FFB32C`)
  - West: Teal operational stroke (`#06C6AE`)
- **Diagonal Solar Flux Points**: Small 2.2px accent circles in coral, teal, amber, and sky blue.
- **Wordmark**: Dark navy (`#151A2B`) "SunPower" with brand plum "OS" (`#714B67`).

SVG Component implementation: `apps/web/src/components/marketing/sunpower-logo.tsx`.
Available variants: `SunpowerLogo` (full wordmark with optional tagline) and `SunpowerMark` (compact standalone icon).

---

## 3. Brand Voice and Tone

- **Confident & Business-Oriented**: Speaks directly to solar EPC business owners, project managers, and technical leads.
- **Pragmatic & Practical**: Replaces empty SaaS buzzwords with specific solar terminology: sanctioned load, rooftop usable area, DC/AC ratio, temperature-adjusted string bounds ($V_{oc,\text{cold}}$ and $V_{mp,\text{hot}}$), 13.8% composite Indian GST, and PM Surya Ghar subsidy tiers.
- **Truthful & Transparent**: Zero mock business data in production, clearly separating live features from upcoming roadmap items.
