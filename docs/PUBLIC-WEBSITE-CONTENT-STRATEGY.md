# SunPowerOS Public Website Content Strategy

## 1. Core Narrative Architecture

The public homepage guides visitors through three critical business questions:
1. **What is SunPowerOS?** $\to$ The purpose-built operating platform for solar EPC contractors and residential/commercial installers.
2. **How does it help my solar business?** $\to$ Connects the disconnected steps between initial customer enquiries, site surveys, engineering calculations, BOM compilation, quotations, and confirmed sales orders.
3. **How can I start using it?** $\to$ Self-service workspace registration via `Start Free` in under 60 seconds.
