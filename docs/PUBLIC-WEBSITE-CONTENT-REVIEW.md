# Public Website Content Strategy & Veracity Review

**Product:** SunPowerOS  
**Publisher:** SunPower Solutions  
**Review Type:** Content Veracity, Legal Compliance & Narrative Quality

## 1. Core Narrative Principles

SunPowerOS is positioned as the purpose-built operating system for solar EPC businesses in India. The marketing content avoids Silicon Valley SaaS clichés, hyperbolic guarantees, and unsupported technical claims. Instead, the copy focuses on actual solar engineering concepts, Indian regulatory norms, and genuine business workflows.

## 2. Claim Sanitization Matrix

### 2.1 Goods and Services Tax (GST)
- **Previous Unsound Claim**: "Universal 13.8% composite Indian GST."
- **Issue**: While the 70% goods @ 12% and 30% services @ 18% formula yields an effective 13.8% rate under Notification No. 24/2018-Central Tax (Rate), different projects, commercial setups, or pure supply orders apply standard 12% or 18% rates.
- **Sanitized Truthful Claim**: "Dynamic Multi-Rate GST (Applicable GST based on configured tax rules: 70% goods @ 12% + 30% services @ 18% (13.8% composite) or standard slabs)."

### 2.2 Payment Milestones
- **Previous Unsound Claim**: "Statutory 4-stage payment milestones (20-40-30-10)."
- **Issue**: The 20-40-30-10 milestone split is an industry convention, not a statutory legislative requirement.
- **Sanitized Truthful Claim**: "Configurable contract milestone payment schedules (e.g., booking advance, structure delivery, installation completion, net meter commissioning)."

### 2.3 Data Security & Tenant Isolation
- **Previous Unsound Claim**: "100% secure with zero data leakage."
- **Issue**: Absolute security claims create unhedged legal liability and misrepresent cybersecurity realities.
- **Sanitized Truthful Claim**: "PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) enforcing database engine-level tenant and branch scoping on all tables with verified query boundary enforcement."

### 2.4 Self-Service Onboarding
- **Previous Unsound Claim**: "Create your organization in 60 seconds with no credit card required."
- **Issue**: Registration requires verifying company details, state jurisdiction, and account credentials.
- **Sanitized Truthful Claim**: "Create your organization in minutes • Indian Rupee (INR) & Asia/Kolkata timezone standard."

## 3. Product Preview & Sample Data Policy

To prevent confusion between marketing samples and live production customer records:
1. Every browser preview frame contains a prominent `Illustrative Product Preview` badge in its toolbar.
2. All customer references use generic illustrative identifiers:
   - `Sample Residential Rooftop` (Civil Lines, Jaipur)
   - `Example Commercial Rooftop` (Industrial Area Phase 2, Bhiwadi)
   - `Sample Rooftop Customer` (Sector 48, Solar Park, Gurugram)
   - `Sample Site, South Extension, New Delhi`
3. Contact details use non-routable placeholder patterns (`+91 98110 XXXXX`, `sample.client@example.com`).
