# SolarOS PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Engine

## 1. Statutory Scheme Background

The Indian Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) administers the **PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana** for grid-connected residential rooftop solar plants.

## 2. Calculation Rules & Slabs

The subsidy function calculates financial assistance as follows:

| Plant Capacity ($C_{\text{kW}}$) | Customer Type | Central Subsidy Amount |
|---|---|---|
| $C \le 1.0\text{ kW}$ | RESIDENTIAL | ₹30,000 flat |
| $1.0 < C \le 2.0\text{ kW}$ | RESIDENTIAL | ₹60,000 flat |
| $C \ge 3.0\text{ kW}$ | RESIDENTIAL | ₹78,000 (Maximum Cap) |
| Any Capacity | COMMERCIAL / INDUSTRIAL | ₹0 (Ineligible) |

## 3. Disbursal & Commercial Presentation

- Quotations present the Gross EPC Price, the Composite GST, and the deducted PM Surya Ghar subsidy clearly to the customer.
- Contracts detail the customer's net payable contribution vs. the DBT claim paperwork handled by the EPC company on the National Portal.
